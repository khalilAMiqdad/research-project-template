# Decision Log

Every decision that affects **methodology, data, analysis, security or deliverables** gets a
record, so that anyone can later understand *what* was decided, *why* and *by whom*.

1. Copy [DR-000_template.md](DR-000_template.md) to `DR-<NNN>_<short_title>.md`.
2. Add it through a PR; the approver(s) named in the record approve the PR.
3. Add a row below. Decisions are never deleted — a changed decision gets a new record that *supersedes* the old one.

| ID | Date | Title | Area | Status | Decided by | Supersedes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| DR-001 | [YYYY-MM-DD] | Adopt repository structure and data-security policy | Governance | Accepted | [PROJECT_LEAD] | — |
