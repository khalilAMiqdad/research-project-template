# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Members of the [PROJECT_NAME] team commit to a respectful, inclusive and professional
collaboration, regardless of background, gender, age, nationality, religion, disability,
seniority or language.

## Expected behaviour

- Review **work, not people**. Critique methods, code and text constructively and specifically.
- Assume good intent; ask before concluding.
- Credit contributions accurately (authorship follows [AUTHORSHIP_POLICY], e.g. CRediT roles).
- Respect participants: protect their privacy and dignity in every file, comment and discussion.
- Uphold research integrity: no fabrication, falsification, selective reporting or
  undisclosed deviations from the protocol or analysis plan.
- Communicate in English in shared GitHub spaces unless the whole thread agrees otherwise;
  Arabic is welcome in Arabic-language documents and fieldwork materials.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, insults, discriminatory remarks, intimidation, publishing others' private
information, pressure to alter results, or retaliation against someone who raised a concern.

## Research-integrity concerns

Concerns about data manipulation, undisclosed analysis changes or ethics breaches may be
raised with [PROJECT_LEAD], or — if the concern involves the Project Lead — with
[INSTITUTIONAL_INTEGRITY_OFFICE]. Reports are handled confidentially.

## Enforcement

The Project Lead reviews reports and may respond with a private conversation, a written
warning, removal of repository access, or referral to institutional procedures.
